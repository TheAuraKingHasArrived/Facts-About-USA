Welcome to the USA

The United States of America is a federal republic comprised of 50 states, a federal district (Washington, D.C.), and several territories.

Quick Stats

Capital: Washington, D.C.

Population: ~345 Million

Currency: US Dollar ($)

Statue of Liberty

Located on Liberty Island in New York Harbor, this monument represents freedom and democracy worldwide.

Grand Canyon

A massive, steep-sided canyon carved by the Colorado River in Arizona, famous for its overwhelming size and colorful landscape.

Independence Day

The nation celebrates its birth on July 4, 1776, marking the adoption of the Declaration of Independence.
